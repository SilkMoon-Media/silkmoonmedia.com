05 / AI

AI built into how we work

We use AI across the studio to design, build, test and launch, with a person making the final calls.

  • Computer players

    Computer players that follow every table’s rules, so there is always a game ready, even when friends are away.

  • Code and testing

    AI helps us write, review and test code, so ideas reach players sooner.

  • Languages

    AI helps us draft translations, so each title can reach new languages sooner. We plan to add native-speaker review, language by language.

  • Art and ideas

    AI helps us explore concepts, art directions and copy. Nothing ships without human sign-off.

  • Faster marketing

    AI helps test campaign ideas and turn one launch into content for every channel.

  • A person in charge

    AI speeds up the work. The final call on design, quality and player safety is made by a person, not by AI.
