You can ask us to delete your Lunar Party Games account and the data linked to it at any time. You don't need to reinstall the app to ask.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Paid plans are billed by Apple or Google. Cancel first, in your store account (steps below), or you will keep being charged.

How to delete your account

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Lunar Party Games.
  2. Go to Settings, then Privacy.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account, then confirm.

This deletes your account and its data from the app on your device and from our servers.

Option 2: By email

If you can't delete in the app, email support@silkmoonmedia.com with the subject "Delete my account". Please include:

  • your player ID (in the app: Settings › Player ID › Copy);
  • a deletion code, if the app gives you one in Settings (codes work for 7 days);
  • if you saved your account with a sign-in, the email address or sign-in you used (Apple or Google). Write from that email address if you can;
  • whether you used iPhone/iPad or Android.

We use these details to check the account is yours, so no one else can delete it. If we can't link you to an account — for example, because you no longer have the app and never saved your account with a sign-in — we will tell you. An account that was never saved with a sign-in and never used to buy anything is erased automatically after 12 months without use.

Please don't send passwords, card numbers, ID documents or receipts with card details.

We will reply to confirm we got your request, complete the deletion within 30 days, and tell you when it's done.

What we delete

When your account is deleted, we delete from our servers and from your device:

  • your account and any sign-in details, player ID, display name, profile colour and settings;
  • your coins, items, unlocks and plan benefits in the app;
  • your game rounds, stats, hand history, saved tables, cup and leaderboard entries;
  • any gameplay stats you chose to share;
  • your daily claim history and any limits, breaks or reminders you set;
  • your Family Room memberships, room names and block list;
  • your display photo (it is only ever stored on your device);
  • your customer record at the service that checks purchases for us (we ask them to delete it).

Chat messages aren't saved, so there are none to delete, except reported messages (see below).

Coins and items can't be restored after deletion, including ones you bought.

On your device: after Delete account, the app keeps a few small records on that device: today's free-coin and ad-coin claims, whether the welcome gift was already given, the under-age lock if one was set, and some display settings (such as the last game tab you used). This stops a new account on the same device from claiming the same free rewards twice, and keeps the age lock in place. These records hold no name or contact details. To remove them, uninstall the app. Your app data isn't included in iCloud, computer or Android cloud backups.

Data held by Apple and Google: your purchase and subscription history in the App Store or Google Play is held by them, not us, and isn't deleted by this request. Ask Apple or Google directly.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account or the app does not cancel a paid plan.

  • iPhone or iPad: open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Lunar Party Games, then tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose Lunar Party Games, then tap Cancel subscription.

Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can also ask us to delete specific data without closing your account. Email support@silkmoonmedia.com with your player ID and tell us what you want removed. Depending on your version of the app, you can also do some of this yourself in Settings, for example remove your display photo, clear your stats and hand history (your coins stay), or turn off Share gameplay stats.
