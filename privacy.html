This policy explains what personal data SilkMoon Media LLC ("SilkMoon", "we", "us") collects through the Lunar Party Games app (the "App") and our website silkmoonmedia.com, why we use it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

Some features described here may not be in every version of the App. Where a feature isn't offered, we don't collect the data that goes with it.

2. The App is for adults only

The App is for people 18 or older, or the age of majority where they live if that is higher. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under that age.

Age check. When you first open the App, it asks for your date of birth. The App uses it only to work out whether you are old enough. We never store your date of birth. We keep only the result (old enough, or not) and when you answered.

Under-age lock. If the answer shows you are under age, the App locks on that device, and on your account where it is stored on our servers, so the answer can't be changed and retried. The lock stays on the device even if you delete your account (see section 10). If you believe someone under age is using the App, email us and we will close the account.

"Share gameplay stats" is off unless you turn it on. You can turn it on or off at any time in the App's settings. If you turn it on, the App sends us, linked to your account, information such as:

It never includes your date of birth, chat messages, name, photo or advertising ID. We use it only to balance and improve the games.

5. Legal bases

Where the law requires a legal basis for using personal data (for example in the UK and European Economic Area), we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the App you asked for, including your account, coins, purchases and games.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep players safe, stop cheating and fraud, secure the App and fix problems, in ways that don't override your rights.
  • Consent: for "Share gameplay stats" and for optional notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in the App's settings.
  • Legal obligation: to keep tax and accounting records and to answer lawful requests from authorities.

7. Ads

The free version of the App may show ads from Google AdMob. Paid plans don't show ads. You can also choose to watch an ad for free coins.

Ads are not personalised. We set AdMob to show non-personalised ads only. Ads are chosen from the App itself and your general area, not from a profile of what you do in other apps or websites. We don't track you for ads, and we don't ask for permission to track you.

To show and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, Google still receives some data, such as your IP address (and a general location worked out from it), device identifiers and details, app details, and how you interact with ads. See Google's privacy policy for how Google uses this data.

We block ads for real-money gambling and alcohol. If an ad looks wrong, tell us at support@silkmoonmedia.com, or use the App's ad report option where available.

8. Chat, Family Rooms and other players

  • Messages and reactions you send in a Family Room are seen by the players in that room at the time. Don't share personal details in chat.
  • Display names, room names and chat are checked automatically for offensive words. Filters aren't perfect, so don't share personal details in names or chat.
  • Chat messages aren't saved. They pass through live to the players in the room and are not stored on our servers.
  • If a message is reported, we keep the reported message, with the messages around it, who sent them and when, for 12 months to review the report and keep players safe.
  • You can block other players.
  • Your display photo is never shown to other players.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the App: Settings › Privacy › Delete account. If you can't use the App, email support@silkmoonmedia.com with your player ID, and a deletion code from the App if it gives you one. Full details are on our Account Deletion page.

When you delete your account, we delete your account and data within 30 days, including from our servers, and ask our purchase service to delete your customer record. We keep only the records described in section 9.

What stays on your device after deletion. To stop the same free rewards being claimed twice and to keep the age lock in place, the App keeps a few small records on that device: today's free-coin and ad-coin claims, whether the welcome gift was already given, and the under-age lock if one was set. It also keeps some display settings, such as the last game tab you used. These hold no name or contact details. Uninstalling the App removes them.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in the App Store or Google Play.

11. Your rights and choices

  • In the App: depending on your version of the App, you can turn notifications and "Share gameplay stats" on or off, hide your online status, remove your display photo, block players and delete your account.
  • On your device: turn off notifications, and reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • By email: ask to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, or object to or limit some uses. We may need to check that the account is yours, for example with your player ID and a deletion code from the App, or by your sign-in email. We will reply within 45 days. If we need more time, as the law allows, we will tell you why. You can appeal a refusal by replying to our answer, and we will tell you the result.
  • Complaints: you can complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Security

The App talks to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We limit access to personal data to people and providers who need it to run the App. Payment details stay with Apple and Google. No system is perfectly secure.

13. International transfers
